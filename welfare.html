Lifelong Social Security For Every Member Family

From student scholarships to death donation aid and medical relief, our welfare funds stand with you through every stage of life.

SURPLUS SHARING

Annual Dividends

Direct return of the society's annual net surplus back to our shareholding members based on their share capital equity.

How to Claim: Distributed annually during the Annual General Meeting (AGM) via dividend vouchers redeemable at any CRB branch or credited to your savings account.
BEREAVEMENT SECURITY

Death Donation Fund (අවමංගල්‍යාධාර)

Immediate financial relief granted without red tape to support funeral expenses:

  • • Rs. 50,000/- upon the unfortunate demise of an active member.
  • • Rs. 25,000/- upon the demise of a registered dependent (spouse or child).
Disbursed within 24 hours of notification upon submission of death certificate.
FUTURE GENERATION

Scholarships & Educational Awards

Encouraging academic excellence among children of member families:

  • • Cash stipends and school stationery kits for Grade 5 scholarship qualifiers.
  • • Distinction award medals and bursaries for G.C.E. O/L 9A top achievers.
  • • Higher education university admission grant for G.C.E. A/L achievers.
HEALTH SOLIDARITY

Medical & Critical Illness Relief

Direct non-repayable humanitarian financial assistance:

  • • Subsidies for cardiac bypass surgeries & stent placements.
  • • Financial grants for oncology treatments and chemotherapy drugs.
  • • Full funding for intraocular lens (IOL) cataract surgeries for senior members.

How It Works

How to Become a Co-operative Member

Any citizen residing or working within our operating area can join with full democratic ownership.

01

Check Residency

Must reside or operate a business within the Negombo division or adjoining area councils.

02

Fill Application

Complete the membership form supported by two existing member endorsements.

03

Subscribe Shares

Purchase minimum share capital (Rs. 100 per share, min 10 shares) to establish legal voting rights.

04

Receive Passbook

Receive official Member Certificate, CRB passbook, and entitlement to lifelong welfare benefits.
