Our Legacy

Our Heritage & Background

Established under the Co-operative Societies Law No. 5 of 1972 during the national co-operative restructuring, the Demo Multi-Purpose Co-operative Society Ltd. was born from the unification of several village co-operatives.

Over five decades of devoted service, our society has expanded into a community anchor: 14 Co-operative Rural Bank branches, 18 Co-op City supermarkets, 2 modern automated fuel filling stations, a 400-seat reception banquet hall, and an extensive social welfare network serving over 35,000 member households.
