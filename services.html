4 Core Wings
Banking Wing

Co-operative Rural Bank (CRB)

Providing safe savings, fixed deposits, affordable self-employment loans, and confidential gold pawning with state vault protection across 14 branches.

Community Banking

Safe Savings & Micro-Enterprise Credit

14 Branches across Negombo and Katunayake protecting deposits under government cooperative statutory regulations.

Savings Accounts

  • • Regular Savings: Calculated on daily balance at 4.50% AER. Min deposit Rs. 500/-.
  • • Kekulu Children's Savings: 6.50% AER with gift schemes, school supplies, and exam excellence awards.
  • • Senior Citizens Savings: 7.00% AER for members aged 55+ with monthly interest payout.

Fixed Deposits & Growth

  • • 3 Months Term: 8.50% AER for flexible cash management.
  • • 6 Months Term: 9.75% AER with monthly payout flexibility.
  • • 12 Months Term: 11.00% AER, fully backed by the Department of Co-operatives.
  • • 24 Months Term: 11.50% AER for guaranteed long-term gains.

Instant Pawning & Loans

  • • Ran Naya (Gold Pawning): Highest market advance for 22/24k gold. Low 1.15% per Rs. 100/mo.
  • • Micro & Self-Employment Loans: Concessionary rates for small businesses & women entrepreneurs.
  • • Agro & Fisheries Loans: Seasonal credit facilities for fishermen and crop farmers.
  • • Salaried Instant Loans: Hassle-free credit for public & private sector employees.

Retail Chain

Consumer & Retail Chain (Co-op City)

Consumer Supermarkets

18 Co-op City Outlets

Modern, air-conditioned supermarket experience offering rice, sugar, flour, canned goods, spices, and household essentials at guaranteed fair and state-controlled prices.

Farm-to-Shelf Freshness

Direct bulk procurement from local farmer societies eliminates middlemen, guaranteeing fair prices for producers and affordable fresh fruits and vegetables for households.

Aswesuma & Disaster Relief

Designated official distribution hub for government Aswesuma welfare ration packages and rapid emergency food supply during weather and economic crises.
