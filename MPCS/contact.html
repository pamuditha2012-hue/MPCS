Help & Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions regarding membership, banking, and services.

Who is eligible to open a Co-operative Rural Bank account?

Any Sri Lankan citizen residing anywhere can open Regular Savings, Kekulu Children's Savings, or Fixed Deposit accounts at our CRB branches. You do not need to be a shareholder to save with us; however, shareholding members enjoy higher welfare privileges.

Are my deposits safe in the Co-operative Rural Bank?

Yes. Demo MPCS operates under the statutory supervision of the Department of Co-operative Development (Western Province) under Co-operative Societies Act No. 5 of 1972, with regular provincial government audits and dedicated statutory reserve funds.

How do I book the Co-op Reception Hall?

You can visit the Reception Hall booking desk at No. 122, Colombo Road, Negombo or call 031-2223458. Active members receive significant concessional rates for weddings and private functions.

How can registered suppliers participate in tenders?

Suppliers can download the bidding specifications on our News & Tenders page or collect tender dossiers from the General Manager's Office by paying the non-refundable tender fee before the stipulated deadline.
